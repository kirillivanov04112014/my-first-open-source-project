# 🌍 Code of Conduct

Welcome to our open-source community!

We want everyone to feel welcome, respected and safe.

## 🤝 Our Principles

- Be respectful and friendly.
- Help other contributors.
- Respect different opinions.
- Communicate politely.
- Support beginners.

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or bullying.
- Insults and offensive language.
- Discrimination.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions.

## 💬 Community Guidelines

1. Be kind to others.
2. Give constructive feedback.
3. Keep discussions useful.
4. Respect everyone's work.

## ❤️ Our Goal

Let's create a friendly and welcoming community where everyone can learn and contribute!
